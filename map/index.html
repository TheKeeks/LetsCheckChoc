The 3D surface uses the existing 16 m signed elevation grid around Fishers Island and The Race, built from NOAA topo-bathymetry. Vertical scale is exaggerated for visibility. Outside the survey footprint, the viewer stops instead of inventing depths.

NOAA NCEI relief uses the service’s finest available survey mosaic (about 3 m where 1/9 arc-second CUDEM is available). Imagery © Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics and the GIS User Community; source detail and dates vary. USGS The National Map provides topography. Imagery and relief are requested as tiles for the current view, never saved into the repository.

Sharper textures do not add elevation measurements. Regional GFS-Wave / ECMWF fields and wind keep their native resolution; directional marks illustrate the forecast, not a high-resolution current simulation. Refraction paths are the existing research model, snapped to the nearest available 2-second period and 5° direction. Bathymetry-following overlays are a viewing aid, not underwater waves. This is a surf exploration map, not a navigation chart.
