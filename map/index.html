Data and credits

Sea floor and island: NOAA NGS 2023 topobathy lidar (1 m), NOAA NCEI multibeam surveys (0.4–2 m, MLLW shifted to NAVD88), NOAA CUDEM (3 m) and USGS 3DEP lidar, built into 0.8 m and 4 m grids for the Fishers Island viewer. Beyond Fishers: the Sound Check 16 m survey grid and its 144 m refraction grid. Heights are NAVD88.

Wave height: the Sound Check refraction model: each GFS-Wave (or ECMWF) swell train at buoy 44097, times the model's refraction coefficient for its period and direction, trains added as energy, capped at 0.78 × depth.

Swell motion: wave rays traced across the same depth grids (144 m across the Sound, 32 m around Fishers); streaks follow the rays' direction and slow to the waves' group speed in shallow water. More streaks where the rays bunch up (focused energy).

Map (← Map): the Sound Check's own map: GFS-Wave and ECMWF across the Atlantic, any day since 2016, and Drop a wave.

Live map layers: Esri World Imagery and Ocean Base (Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics, GEBCO, NOAA); NOAA Office of Coast Survey ENC chart display.
