Model vs Buoy — is the forecast telling the truth?

Every 2 hours the site records what the buoy actually measured and what the forecast model claimed for that same hour. Each chart below is a timeline (oldest on the left, newest on the right — faint vertical lines are midnight). The closer the dashed/dotted model lines hug the solid buoy line, the more the forecast can be trusted.

Wave period — seconds between waves (buoy energy period vs model mean period)
