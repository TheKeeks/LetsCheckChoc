Wave Spectra

Wave Spectra

What is this?

A decomposition of the buoy's raw wave spectrum into three interpretable rows:

  • Primary Swell — the dominant long-period wave train the spectrum can isolate, typically a distant-storm groundswell.
  • Wind Waves — the shorter-period train driven by the local wind at the buoy.
  • Significant Hs — the total significant wave height of the sea state (mean of the highest one-third of waves); the combined energy of every ripple, chop, and groundswell currently present.

Wave Energy Spectrum

What is this?

Spectral energy density plotted as a function of wave period. Each peak is a distinct wave train present in the sea state; peak height indicates that train's share of total energy.

What is this?

Predicted water-level curve derived from NOAA CO-OPS harmonic constants for the selected station. Peaks mark high tides, troughs mark low tides, and the dashed vertical line marks the current moment.
